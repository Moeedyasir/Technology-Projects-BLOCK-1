   - Binary

     (39 / 2) = 19 remainder = 1
     (19 / 2) = 9 remainder = 1
     (9 / 2) = 4 remainder = 1
     (4 / 2) = 2 remainder = 0
     (2 / 2) = 1 remainder = 0
     (1 / 2) = 0 remainder = 1
     10011100
   - Octal
     (156 / 8) = 19 remainder = 4
     (19 / 8) = 2 remainder = 3
     (2 / 8) = 0.25 remainder = 2
     234

   - Hexadecimal
   after MSD the letter A becomes 10 and F becomes 15 to the least significant digit.
   Letter starts from 10
     (156 / 16) = 9.75 remainder = 12 - C
